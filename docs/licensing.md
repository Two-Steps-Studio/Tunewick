# Licensing & Rights

> Status: **v0.1 working model — NOT legal advice.** Every item marked ⚖️ requires validation
> by a lawyer specialised in Polish/EU copyright and platform law (Guidon: "Legal review").
> The product must not launch publicly until those items are resolved.

## 1. Scope of the MVP content model

Decided 2026-10-05: content comes from **independent artists (and their own micro-labels) who
upload music they control**. No commercial label/major catalog, no user-uploaded third-party
content, no covers without clearance, no DJ mixes of other people's tracks in MVP.

## 2. Rights layers in a streamed track

| Layer | Holder (typical indie case) | What Tunewick needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Master / phonogram** | The artist or their micro-label (producer of the recording) | A license to reproduce and make available, granted in the Artist Terms on upload. |
| **Composition (music + lyrics)** | Songwriters; if members of a collective management organization (CMO), the CMO manages the relevant rights | ⚖️ If any author is a **ZAiKS** member (or a foreign CMO member), Tunewick likely needs a ZAiKS license for online making available and must report usage, even when the artist uploads the track themselves. If no author is a CMO member, the artist can license directly. |
| **Performers' related rights** | Performers | ⚖️ Possible remuneration claims via **STOART** / **SAWP** depending on use type and membership. |
| **Phonogram producers' related rights** | Producer | ⚖️ **ZPAV** relevance for indie self-releases to be confirmed. |
| **Artwork, photos** | Artist / photographer | License in Artist Terms; uploader warrants they have rights. |
| **Metadata** | Artist | License to display, index and use in discovery/graph. |

## 3. Upload-time rights declaration

Every release submission requires a declaration stored immutably in `rights_declarations`:

1. "I own or control the master recording rights for all tracks" (yes/no).
2. "I own or control the composition rights, or I declare the CMO memberships below" — per release: list of authors' CMO memberships (ZAiKS, other, none, unknown).
3. Samples and interpolations: none / cleared (with description) — uncleared samples are rejected.
4. AI content: human / AI-assisted / AI-generated (see §6).
5. Territories (default: worldwide, artist may restrict).
6. Acceptance of the Artist Terms version.

The declaration is evidence, not proof. Moderation and duplicate/fingerprint checks run anyway.

## 4. Artist Terms (outline for the lawyer) ⚖️

- Non-exclusive license to store, transcode, stream, display, and use metadata for discovery, in the selected territories.
- Artist keeps ownership; can withdraw a release (takes effect within a defined period; already-accrued payouts unaffected).
- Warranties: rights ownership, no infringement, accuracy of CMO declarations and AI declaration.
- Indemnity for false declarations.
- Payout terms when paid plans exist: user-centric model (D2); until then, no payouts and no revenue (beta, free) — stated plainly.
- Promotional use: soundcheck excerpts, artwork in Tunewick marketing, opt-out available.
- No DRM commitment; streaming-only (no downloads) in MVP.
- Quality: Tunewick stores the master unaltered and never markets it beyond its verified properties.

## 5. Takedown and disputes (EU DSA-aligned) ⚖️

1. **Notice** via a public form: claimant identity, work, location (URL), basis, good-faith statement.
2. **Assessment** by a moderator; manifestly infringing content restricted quickly.
3. **Notification** of the uploader with a **statement of reasons**.
4. **Counter-notice** by the uploader; decision; restore or keep down.
5. **Appeal** path; records retained.
6. **Repeat infringers**: account restrictions after repeated valid notices.
7. Transparency reporting as required for the platform size category.

## 6. AI content labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Human | Composed and performed by people; tools like DAWs/effects don't change this. |
| AI-assisted | AI used for parts (e.g. stems separation, mastering assist, generated elements) with human authorship. |
| AI-generated | Substantially generated by AI. |
| Unknown | Not declared (only legacy/admin cases). |

Rules: labels come from the artist's declaration and are shown on track/release pages. Tunewick
never assigns "AI-generated" by accusation. A moderator can request clarification and record a
note; an automated detector, if added later, can only open a review, never change the label
publicly. ⚖️ The EU AI Act transparency obligations for AI-generated content are to be checked.

## 7. Territories

MVP availability: Poland and the EU, with the artist's territory selection respected. Playback
token issuance checks territory from a coarse IP country lookup (no storage of the IP).
Expansion requires re-checking CMO arrangements per country ⚖️.

## 8. Events and venue data

Events are created by artists, venues or admins. Only real events are published. External
event data (scraping or third-party feeds) is not used without a license/terms review.
